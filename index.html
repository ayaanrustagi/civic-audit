About the meeting dates

Three different dates appear around each meeting, and they are not the same thing. The date displayed on a Citizen Portal page can differ from the meeting date: the page for the 15 October 2026 Leander City Council meeting (source 11209546) displays 14 October. For Leander ISD pages the itemDate in the page data is the day the video was uploaded, one day after the meeting (for example 2026-10-02 for the 1 October meeting). And one Leander ISD page (source 11346006) was generated from the 1 October BoardBook minutes PDF, not from the video, so for that page the record and the source are the same document. That 1 October BoardBook minutes PDF is also the record quoted for the 1 October spot checks. Dates in this report are meeting dates taken from the official minutes or agenda.

How the audit worked

Review funnel
  1. 01
    Extract atomic claims

    Split the public meeting summaries and timeline text into individual claims. Each claim points to the exact published wording it came from. The systematic article stage is pending.

  2. 02
    Retrieve the record

    Match each claim to the relevant minutes, agenda or meeting video. Swagit and CivicClerk cover the Leander city meetings. YouTube and BoardBook cover Leander ISD. Cedar Park uses Swagit, agendas and a local Whisper transcript. Captions help locate moments in the video.

  3. 03
    Run two independent checks

    GPT-6.1 Terra (high reasoning) checked the claims against the matching record. GPT-6 Astra, with Claude Sonnet finishing the groups left unchecked when the ChatGPT usage limit stopped the run rechecked every group the first checker flagged plus a fixed random 25% of the rest (seed 20261116), without seeing the first verdicts.

  4. 04
    Review the reference labels

    Fable (AI) reviewed all 234 claims either checker called contradicted and a random sample of 60 claims both called supported. An error requires contradictory evidence in the official record. Every label still needs Ayaan’s confirmation.

  5. 05
    Measure agreement and accuracy

    Use an item-cluster bootstrap that resamples whole summaries or timeline segments because claims in the same item are related. Cohen’s kappa measures agreement between the checkers. Precision is the share of checker flags that held up in review. Recall is the share of reviewed errors each checker flagged.

Strengths and limits

What Citizen Portal does well
  • The first checker raised no flag on 90.4% of the claims it read (that figure includes unclear and not found, which are not successes either).
  • In the random sample of claims both checkers called supported, the reference review found 0 of 29 = 0.0% (95% upper bound 11.3%, by item) errors.
  • Claim types the first checker flagged least often (under 5%, at least 20 claims): characterization, date. Votes and amounts were flagged more often.
  • Of the confirmed errors, 97 of 128 are misspelled names (E1), many of them sound-alike spellings of the kind speech recognition produces.
  • Every meeting page links the summary to a time in the video and carries the notice that the text is AI generated, which is what made this check possible.
Limits of this study
  • Small sample: 14 meeting pages, 4 bodies, one season. Intervals are wide.
  • The reference labels were made by an AI reviewer and await Ayaan's confirmation. The supported sample is 0 of 29 = 0.0% (95% upper bound 11.3%, by item).
  • Two candidate number errors from our local Whisper transcript of the Cedar Park 22 October 2026 meeting were withdrawn after re-transcription with three Whisper models matched Citizen Portal's 4,317 and 2,500. A human listen to the official video is still advised.
  • Some claims rest on machine captions and video rather than on a signed document.
  • Minutes not yet posted for: Cedar Park City Council 2026-10-22; Cedar Park City Council 2026-11-05; Leander City Council 2026-11-05; Leander City Council 2026-11-10; Leander Planning and Zoning Commission 2026-10-22. Those meetings were checked against the agenda and video only.
  • Articles: 79 listed, collected: 79. The share of articles with a Major error is 10 of 79.
  • Pages can change after an error is reported; snapshots are dated.
